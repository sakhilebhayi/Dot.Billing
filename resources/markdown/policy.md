_Last updated: 6 August 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our"), the company responsible for Dot.Billing, collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use Dot.Billing and the wider Dot Ecosystem it connects to. It is written to align with South Africa's **Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA")**.

## 1. Who we are

BluePin Inc is the responsible party for the personal information Dot.Billing processes. Our Information Officer can be reached at [privacy@infodot.co.za](mailto:privacy@infodot.co.za) for any question, request, or concern about your personal information.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account information** — your name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), via your Dot Ecosystem account.
- **Team and billing information** — your team's active plan, subscription status, invoices, payments, and credits.
- **Payment references** — Dot.Billing does not process card payments itself; it stores Stripe identifiers as references to transactions actually handled by Stripe. We do not store your full card number.
- **Usage data** — per-platform usage your team's plan is metered against.
- **Technical information** — IP address, browser and device information, and session activity, collected automatically for security and to keep you signed in.

## 3. Why we process your information

We process personal information to:

- create and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you sign in;
- let you sign in once and move between connected Dot Ecosystem platforms without re-entering your credentials;
- track your team's plan, generate and display invoices, and process payments through Stripe;
- monitor usage against your plan and send billing alerts; and
- meet our legal and accounting obligations, and respond when you contact us.

## 4. AI-generated spend commentary

Where enabled, Dot.Billing can generate AI commentary summarising your team's spend and usage trends. This is generated from your team's own billing and usage data and is shown only to your team.

## 5. Ecosystem single sign-on

When you use another Dot Ecosystem platform to sign in to Dot.Billing (or vice versa), a short-lived, single-use authentication token confirms who you are without exposing your password to the connected platform.

## 6. Sharing with payment processors

We share the minimum information necessary with Stripe to process payments and issue invoices on your team's behalf. Stripe processes that information under its own privacy policy and terms.

## 7. Cookies

Dot.Billing uses a small number of strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect your account and forms from cross-site attacks. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies) for the full list and what each one does.

## 8. How long we keep your information

We keep your account and billing records for as long as your account is active, and for as long afterward as required by tax and accounting law. If you delete your account, we remove your personal account information within a reasonable period, subject to those legal retention requirements.

## 9. How we protect your information

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Ecosystem sign-on tokens are short-lived and single-use. We apply reasonable technical and organisational security measures appropriate to the information we hold.

## 10. Your rights under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the right to:

- ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, and to access it;
- ask us to correct or delete personal information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, misleading, or obtained unlawfully;
- object, on reasonable grounds, to the processing of your personal information;
- withdraw any consent you've given us, at any time, for a specific purpose;
- ask us not to use your information for direct marketing; and
- lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za) if you believe we have processed your personal information unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer at [privacy@infodot.co.za](mailto:privacy@infodot.co.za).

## 11. Children

Dot.Billing is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent of a parent or guardian as required by POPIA.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll change the "Last updated" date above when we do, and for material changes we'll take reasonable steps to let you know before they take effect.

## 13. Contact us
